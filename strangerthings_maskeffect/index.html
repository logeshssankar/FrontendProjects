STRANGER THINGS

The Upside Down is an alternate dimension that mirrors the town of Hawkins, Indiana, but is filled with darkness, monstrous creatures, and a haunting atmosphere.

The Mind Flayer

The Mind Flayer, also known as the hive mind, is a central element of the storyline. It is depicted as a massive, spider-like entity with telepathic abilities, capable of influencing and controlling others across dimensions.
